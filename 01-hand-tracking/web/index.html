How it works

MediaPipe finds 21 points on your hand in every video frame. The code checks which fingers are up to decide whether you are drawing, picking, erasing or clearing. With SNAP on, a rough circle, rectangle, triangle or straight line turns into a clean shape. Anything else, like a flower, stays exactly as you drew it.

The tracking library and a small hand model (about 8 MB) download from jsDelivr and Google when you press Start. Your camera video is processed on your device and is not uploaded. Get the Python version and code.
